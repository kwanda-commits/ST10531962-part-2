Website Choice  

Name: FC Barcelona PTY LTD 

Organisation Description:  

The name of the business is FC Barcelona. The business offers new and quality retro kits for all sorts of customers at affordable prices therefore accomodating those who have strict budgets. 

 

Reasons for Choosing the Organisation:  

I specifically chose this business because of my interest in football and combating the unavailability of affordable retro kits in South Africa. 

I primarily chose developing this website in order to establish a professional digital presence for FC Barcelona PTY LTD that aligns with the company's mission of “providing the best quality kits and equipment for every young aspiring footballer and providing memorabilia for fans of the sport.  

In today’s digital economy, a website serves as the central hub for brand credibility, customer acquisition, and sales. For a company that supplies football equipment and merchandise, an online platform is essential to reach both local and national markets beyond physical store limitations. 

Website Goals and Objectives: 

The website will help the business reach a wider market and because most people prefer to shop online rather than go to the actual store making it convenient for them. The website will also make it easy for the business to showcase its products and services by helping with marketing and promotions. A website also helps the businesses credibility with customer reviews online. The mission is to provide customers with affordable tech while delivering service and value.

New Features Added (Part 2 – CSS Styling and Responsive Design) 

Following feedback on Part 1, the website was styled with an external CSS stylesheet and made responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile screens. The following features were added: 

• A single external stylesheet (css/styles.css) linked to every page, built around a garnet, azure and gold colour palette that reflects the FC Barcelona brand. 

• A consistent type scale, using a bold display typeface for headings and a clean sans-serif for body text. 

• A flexbox header and navigation bar, a CSS Grid layout for the team section and product content, and a two-column layout for the contact page. 

• Responsive breakpoints at 1024px and 640px: the contact layout collapses to a single column, the navigation stacks vertically, and images and embedded content resize for tablet and mobile screens. 

• Corrections carried over from Part 1 feedback: fixed mismatched links to the Track order and Retro jerseys pages, added the missing stylesheet link on the home page, corrected the navigation markup, and fixed the contact form’s email address. 

These changes, along with the corresponding changelog entries, have been committed to the GitHub repository.
